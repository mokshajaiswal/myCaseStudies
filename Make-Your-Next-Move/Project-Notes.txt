MAKE YOUR NEXT MOVE
10-second editable motion study, authored in Tesseract 0.3.1.

Deliverable: Make-Your-Next-Move.mp4 (1920x1080, 30 fps, 300 frames, 10 seconds, H.264/AAC).
Editable document: Make-Your-Next-Move.tsrct, with embedded font and audio assets.
Previews: Filmstrip.png and Poster.png.
Assets: Original Space Grotesk variable font and OFL license, plus three locally synthesized WAV accents.
Font source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/spacegrotesk

Story: One small move / Can change everything / Make your next move.
43 native visual layers and 95 editable animation tracks, plus 5 audio placements.
All text, shapes, timing and sound placements remain editable.
No brand or third-party logo is represented.

Sound origins: procedural Tesseract soft-pop (220ms, seed 5), air-whoosh (600ms, seed 12), soft-impact (800ms, seed 3).
Sound is deliberately sparse, with pauses and no music bed or narration.
Measured final AAC: -24.32 LUFS integrated, -10.14 dBTP true peak.

Checks: Native commit/apply and export succeeded. ffprobe confirmed duration, frame count, dimensions, video codec and audio stream. Audio measurement confirmed non-silent cues and peak headroom. Visual review and playback/listening are left to the user under the project policy. No browser verification performed.

Authoring recipe and schema snapshots are retained in .tesseract-work. The build.py recipe uses editable.json as its document/asset scaffold, then generates actions.json; commit the document and apply actions to rebuild. Rendering on this host needs Metal GPU access outside the sandbox.

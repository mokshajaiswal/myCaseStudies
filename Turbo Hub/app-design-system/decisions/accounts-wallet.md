# Accounts & Cards wallet structure

Wallet corner correction: the outer card uses --wallet-card-radius (20px), with the summary's top corners and action's bottom corners derived as outer radius minus the 1px border (19px). The outer card clips its contents so child surfaces cannot cover the border curve. The summary's lower corners retain 16px rounding for the raised-panel treatment. Browser review is left to the user.

The user requested the first card match the supplied comparison: a white balance summary above a pale blue action surface, with the wallet graphic at the right and a circular plus at the left of Add Money. The account list row was replaced with a screen-owned wallet arrangement. The summary has a 90px minimum height, 16px inset, 56px circular artwork, and explicit dark title text. The 60px action region owns its native button and 32px circular plus. Tokens provide spacing and typography; the wallet graphic is replaceable through TurboIcons. Other account cards retain their existing structure. Add Money remains a prototype action without a money flow.

Per the user's explicit request, no browser testing or automated tests were performed. Visual review and interaction testing are left to the user.

The wallet icon now uses genuine Phosphor core 2.1.1 geometry from the published @phosphor-icons/core package, registered in the existing Regular/Bold/Fill tables. TurboIcons maps the semantic wallet name to that provider icon, so it appears in the existing searchable Iconography gallery and respects its weight selector and copy/export tooling. The earlier custom silhouette is removed. Card color and elevation declarations now use named wallet tokens in tokens/semantics.css; surface and icon roles alias the existing DS palette. No browser testing was performed, as requested.

UPI Accounts and Linked Cards now reuse the wallet's screen-owned account summary arrangement, including 16px title/detail text, right-side circular icons and concentric corners. UPI is a summary-only surface; Linked Cards adds a pale blue horizontally scrolling strip with circular Add card and white bank pills. Bank marks are generic text placeholders. Bank and credit-card use registered upstream Phosphor 2.1.1 geometry in all three supported weights; the semantic bank/card mappings expose them in Iconography. The Turbo Hub banner remains unchanged. Manual review remains with the user.

Account card is now extracted to components/account-card.js and .css and registered as turbo-account-card. Summary, Wallet and Linked cards have canonical renderer-backed previews, plus optional detail, empty linked list and long-content examples. Its independent labeled/icon buttons are registered as turbo-account-action, with native disabled, keyboard, hover, active and focus-visible ownership. Source spacing audit: summary inset/gap 16px, detail gap 4px, strip inset 16px horizontal/12px vertical and gap 8px, action inset 16px horizontal/12px vertical with 8px gap. Parent owns external placement; component owns card surfaces and child arrangement; actions own control geometry. Browser and inspector checks remain pending for user review.

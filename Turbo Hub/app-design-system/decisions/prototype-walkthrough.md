## Video controls and stable frame handoffs

The walkthrough adopts the player's registered `presentation:'overlay'`: glass Action buttons for play/pause and expand, elapsed/total time, and the registered Range slider's `variant:'media'`. Controls sit over the phone's lower screen with a dark contrast scrim. The caption remains accessible. The toolbar presentation remains available through the same renderer; both presentations have canonical specimens. The slider's media variant has its own specimen and preserves native pointer/keyboard seeking, disabled and focus states. Its label/output/endpoints are visually hidden, its input allocation is 24px high, track 4px and thumb 10px. The player owns panel padding (24px above, 12px horizontal, 8px below) and action gaps (8px); it does not override registered child internals. Existing glass buttons retain their own 44px hit targets. Play/pause Phosphor fill assets are registered in the existing app icon provider.

The previous load handler replaced the media container with its already-mounted incoming iframe. Removing and reinserting a loaded iframe can restart its browsing context, triggering another load and repeating the cycle. Handoffs now append only new frames and remove only obsolete frames, retaining the visible screen while loading. Late events from obsolete frames are ignored. Snapshot failures surface the player's error/retry state instead of leaving an invisible frame indefinitely.

The overlay's lower edge uses 3.28% of the media height, matching the embedded phone's screen bottom (1.25% centering inset plus the 4.3cqw bezel on its 97.5%-width shell). The earlier 4.5% offset stopped the scrim above the screen bottom and exposed a pale strip. The timeline retains its internal 8px lower inset and 24px input allocation.
